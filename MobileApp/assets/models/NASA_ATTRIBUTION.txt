NASA 3D HARDWARE MODELS

ASTRA uses the following GLB models from NASA's 3D Resources. NASA's catalog states that its models are free to download and use. Follow NASA's Images and Media Usage Guidelines; ASTRA is an independent educational game and is not endorsed by NASA.

- Habitat Demonstration Unit, Parts 1 and 2 — NASA/LaRC/Advanced Concepts Lab
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/habitat-demonstration-unit/
- 70 meter dish — NASA/Ames Research Center
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/70-meter-dish/
- Tracking and Data Relay Satellite (TRDS) (E) — NASA/Christopher R. Meaney
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/tracking-and-data-relay-satellites-trds-e/
- Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (A) — NASA/Christopher R. Meaney
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/lunar-reconnaissance-orbiter-a/
- Space Exploration Vehicle — NASA/LaRC/Advanced Concepts Lab
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/space-exploration-vehicle/
- Apollo Lunar Module — NASA/Michael D. Carbajal
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/apollo-lunar-module/
- International Space Station (ISS) (B) — NASA/Michael D. Carbajal
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/international-space-station-iss-b/

The models are scaled to fit ASTRA's playable set. They are genuine NASA model meshes; some interactive uses (for example, the ISS as a learning reference for oxygen/water/greenhouse systems) are simplified gameplay representations and are labelled in the mission briefing.

NASA media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

All models remain NASA-sourced and NASA usage guidelines apply. Z2 and Perseverance GLBs were downloaded from NASA's 3D Resources pages. NASA models are scaled for gameplay; mission duties and interactions remain game-authored simulations.

Acquisition/processing policy record:
Source category for all models used: NASA. No Local, Sketchfab, or Generated substitute was needed because mission hardware, habitats, rovers, and suits had NASA 3D assets. See Processed/processing-report.json for Blender cleanup/export details and per-model exceptions. The Editor startup importer imports the Resources GLBs with glTFast/Draco; runtime fallback reads original mission hardware GLBs from StreamingAssets.
